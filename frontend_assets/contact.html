Before you write

Ask us anything

01How do I choose the right size?

Our pieces come in XS to 4XL — the size chart lists bust, waist and hip for each. If you are between sizes or unsure how a cut will sit, send us your usual size and height and we'll advise before you order.

02How long does tailoring take?

Every piece is made to order once your payment is confirmed, usually in around two to three weeks. Your account page shows when it moves from tailoring to packed and dispatched.

03How do I pay?

By bank transfer only. The account details are shown at checkout; after transferring, upload a clear photo or PDF of your slip with your order.

04When is my order confirmed?

As soon as we've matched your slip with our bank account, usually within one to two working days. We'll let you know, and your order status changes to Accepted.

05Do you deliver outside Colombo?

Yes, island-wide by courier. Delivery normally takes two to five working days after dispatch, and the delivery charge is shown before you pay.

06Can I exchange for a different size?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery, as long as the piece is unworn, unwashed and in its original packaging. See our exchanges policy.

07What if my piece arrives damaged?

Tell us within 7 days with a few photos and we'll repair, remake or refund it, delivery included.

08Can you make a piece in another colour?

Sometimes, depending on the linen we have. Send us the piece and the colour you have in mind and we'll tell you what's possible.

09How should I care for linen?

Gentle cold wash, no bleach, line dry in the shade and press while slightly damp. Linen softens beautifully with every wash.

10Where can I see my order?

Sign in with the person icon at the top of any page. Your account shows each order and the stage it's at, from payment review to dispatched.
