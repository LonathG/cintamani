ARTIST BIOGRAPHY

Vo Xuan Huy

(1970 - 2016)

Born in 1970 in Thuy Ba Village, Vinh Thuy Commune, Vinh Linh District, Quang Tri Province. He is a dedicated lacquer artist, deeply committed to Vietnamese fine arts and profoundly connected to his homeland. After graduating from the Hue University of Arts, he remained as a lecturer, living and creating in Hue.

With over 15 years of teaching and creating, he continues to push the boundaries of traditional lacquer painting and visual arts. In addition to his work as an artist and educator, he is also a prolific writer, contributing numerous articles on art to various publications.
