01About these terms

These terms apply to every purchase made through the Cintamani Linens website ("Cintamani", "we", "us"). By creating an account or placing an order, you agree to them. We may update these terms from time to time; the version shown here when you place your order is the one that applies to it.

02Your account

  • You need an account to add pieces to your selection and to place an order, so we can keep your delivery details and show the progress of your orders.
  • Please give accurate details. Your name, contact number and delivery address are used to tailor, confirm and deliver your order.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for orders placed through your account; if you think someone else has access to it, contact us straight away.
  • We may suspend an account that is used for fraudulent orders or to misuse the website.

03Orders & pricing

  • All prices are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). The delivery charge, based on parcel weight (see Delivery), is shown on your receipt before you pay.
  • Placing an order is an offer to buy. Your order is accepted only once we have verified your payment; you can follow this on your account page.
  • Each piece is made in limited quantities. If we cannot fulfil an order — for example, if a fabric runs out — we will contact you and offer an alternative or a full refund.
  • If a price is displayed incorrectly because of an error, we will contact you before accepting the order.

04Payment

  • We accept payment by bank transfer only. The account details are shown at checkout.
  • After transferring, upload a clear photo or PDF of your bank slip with your order. Please use your order number as the transfer reference where your bank allows it.
  • We check every slip against our bank account, usually within one to two working days. If the slip is unclear or the amount does not match, we will contact you on the number you provided.
  • Orders with no matching payment within 3 days may be cancelled.

05Tailoring & sizing

  • Pieces are offered in sizes XS to 4XL. Please check your size against the size chart on each product page before ordering; we are happy to advise if you contact us first.
  • Every piece is cut and sewn to order once your payment is accepted. Tailoring usually takes around two to three weeks, depending on the piece and the season. Your account page shows each stage — Accepted, Tailoring, Packed and Dispatched.
  • Once tailoring has begun, an order cannot be cancelled or changed.

06Delivery

  • We deliver island-wide within Sri Lanka by SL Post courier. Once your order is dispatched, a tracking reference appears on your account page.
  • The delivery charge depends on the weight of your parcel (your pieces plus packaging) and follows SL Post courier rates, below. The exact charge for your order is shown on your receipt before you pay.
  • Delivery normally takes two to five working days after dispatch. Delays caused by couriers, weather or public holidays are outside our control.
  • Please make sure someone can receive the parcel at the address given. If a parcel is returned because of an incorrect address or failed delivery, re-delivery may be charged.
  • Inspect your parcel on arrival and tell us within 48 hours if it is damaged or anything is missing.

07Exchanges & returns

Because each piece is tailored for you, we do not offer refunds for a change of mind. We do want you to love what you wear, so:

  • Size exchanges — contact us within 7 days of delivery. The piece must be unworn, unwashed and in its original packaging. Courier charges for exchanges are paid by the customer.
  • Faulty or incorrect pieces — if a piece arrives with a manufacturing fault or is not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days with photos. We will repair, remake or refund it, including delivery costs.
  • Refunds, where they apply, are made by bank transfer to an account you nominate, within 14 working days.

08The nature of linen

Linen is a natural, living fabric, and its character is part of its beauty. Small slubs, variations in weave, gentle creasing and slight differences in shade between the screen and real life are natural and are not considered faults.

Linen softens with every wash. To keep your piece at its best, follow the care label — gentle cold wash, no bleach, line dry in the shade, and press while slightly damp. Damage caused by not following the care instructions is not covered.

  • We collect only what we need to fulfil your orders: your name, email, contact number, delivery address, order history and the bank slips you upload.
  • Your password is stored in encrypted (hashed) form; we can never see it.
  • Your details are shared only with our courier for delivery. We never sell your information.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time, in line with Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act. Order records may be kept where the law requires it.

10Cookies

Like most websites, we store small pieces of information in your browser.

  • Essential — your selection (cart), your sign-in, the delivery details you type at checkout and your cookie choice. The shop can't work without these, so they're always on. They stay on your device and are never used to track you.
  • Analytics — only if you choose Accept all. These cookies (Google Analytics) tell us, anonymously, which pages are visited so we can improve the site. If you choose Essential only, they're not set.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings.
